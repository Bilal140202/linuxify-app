02Nature of the software

Linuxify is in active alpha development. It is distributed as an installable APK that you sideload, not through an app store review process. You should expect: rough edges, occasional crashes, feature changes between releases, and occasional bugs that require reinstalling or resetting the environment. The project tracks its progress publicly and honestly — see the changelog and the repository's issue tracker.

The app runs a full Linux userspace on your device using proot. It does not require or request root access, does not modify your system partition, and confines itself to app-private storage. Nonetheless, you are running real software inside a real environment on your personal device.
