The person
behind the
terminal.

Linuxify is built by one independent developer — self-taught, shipping in public, and writing down everything that breaks along the way.

Sales agent by day.
Software builder by night.

I'm Ansari Mohammad Bilal — an independent software developer and technology writer based in India. I have no formal computer science education and no bootcamp certificate. I started teaching myself to build with AI tooling in 2024, and I've shipped more than twenty apps since — AI agents, desktop utilities, developer tools and open source projects.

I build the way a solo builder has to: small, verifiable steps, everything in version control, and a written record of what works and what doesn't. That habit is exactly why Linuxify is shaped the way it is — every release ships with unit tests, every claim in the app is separated into what is verified and what isn't, and every failure message tries to tell you the truth instead of hiding behind a spinner.

Why Linuxify? Because "run Linux on your phone" has been a toy category for a decade. I wanted the opposite of a toy: a real Ubuntu userspace with a real terminal that respects the user — no accounts, no telemetry, honest limits, and files that stay yours. It's free and open source because that's the only kind of trust a one-person project can genuinely offer.

I also write about this process — what I built, what broke, and what tools earned their keep — in The build log, my newsletter.
